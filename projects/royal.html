About The Project

Befriend the Riches of an
Opulent Lifestyle

Shreedhar Royal shall mark its glory in Vastral in a signature style that's classic yet trending. Inspired by the finesse of architecture, Royal is the treasure-worthy trendsetter at Vastral — three iconic towers rising high on S.P. Ring Road, Nr. Metro Mall, bringing 3 & 4 BHK spacious homes you've always desired for.

The residence seamlessly brings the best of both worlds: a sound foundation and attention to detail, whether it is about quality construction or timely possession. Each apartment offers the liberty of choosing a garden or road-overlooking balcony — so you can choose your view as per your mood.

Designed by Z-Axis Architects and developed by Shreehari Buildcon with RERA No. RAA14636, Royal offers over 30 blissful amenities across an expansive ground floor campus — a community built for families of all sizes.
