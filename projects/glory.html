About The Project

Premium Luxury Residences in Vastral

Shreedhar Glory is a statement of refined urban living — a pure residential project offering premium 2 BHK apartments near Kalhaar Villa, Madhav Farm Road, Vastral. With three iconic towers rising 12 floors, Glory redefines what it means to call Vastral home.

Every detail is crafted with passionate artistry and meticulous attention to perfection. From the decorative foyer echa blocks to the solar rooftop for common areas, Shreedhar Glory is designed not just to impress, but to endure.

As a pure residential project, Glory offers residents the undisturbed peace of a community built entirely around comfortable living — no commercial noise, just the serenity of a thoughtfully planned neighbourhood.
