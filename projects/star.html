About The Project

Visible from Every Direction

Shreedhar Star is a premium residential development offering 3 BHK luxurious flats and shops across four elegantly planned blocks — A, B, C and D — on Devasya School Road, Odhav, Ahmedabad. Every home feels grand because it is, with high-quality architecture visible from all sides.

The project is built with a vision to provide holistic living, ensuring that all your dreams come true at an affordable price. A lush landscaped central courtyard, a pergola garden walkway, and a beautifully designed entrance gate create a community that feels like a world of its own.

With two automatic lifts per block, basement parking, and a comprehensive security system, Shreedhar Star sets a benchmark in Odhav for what it means to live well.
